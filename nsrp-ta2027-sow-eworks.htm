5.6 Planning & Scheduling Requirements
5.6.1 General Requirements
 5.6.1a) TA Contractor-ENERSEV is responsible for planning & scheduling all preparation activities from Detailed Planning Phase through to Post-TA Phase.
  ENERSEV shall submit a Planning & Scheduling Procedure/Plan, aligned w/TA Key Milestones provided by NSRP in Table 4.3.1
5.6.1b) NSRP has prepared Level-3-MechanicalDays/SubstationSD-plan to ENERSEV as Attachment-3 Level-3 integrated SD-schedule for Electrical Package.
  This schedule shall serve as Baseline reference for ENERSEV to develop respective Level-5-Schedules for submission w/Technical Proposal & subsequently Level-6-Schedules during Detailed Planning Phase.
  Level 5 & 6 Schedule shall incorporate complete SoW defined in Attachment 6 Basic WorkPacks, including activities undertaken by Vendors, Licensors, Authorities, 3rd-Parties & NSRP.
5.6.1c) ENERSEV shall exclusively use NSRP's Primavera P6 software system for all scheduling activities.
5.6.1d) ENERSEV shall develop & submit a Level-5-Schedule as part of Technical Proposal. This schedule shall comply w/P6 WBS
  & ensure that Mechanical Days duration for each unit does not exceed Maximum duration specified in Attachment-3 Level-3 integrated SD-schedule for Electrical Package.
5.6.1e) ENERSEV shall identify & highlight Critical & near-Critical paths in Level-5-Schedule to provide a full understanding of all potential risks, constraints & factors that may cause delays to overall TA2-schedule.
5.6.1f) During Detailed Planning Phase, ENERSEV shall be responsible for planning & scheduling all preparation works, including but not limited to following:
+ Developing a schedule to monitor preparation progress & submitting regular progress reports to NSRP. Any delays shall be addressed promptly w/a defined recovery plan.
+ Coordinating w/other TA-Contractors, 3rd-Parties & EPC Projects to collect all necessary info for planning & scheduling.
    All interface points shall be identified & incorporated into Level-6-Schedule to prevent conflicts during execution, under oversight of NSRP.
+ Submitting a detailed Level-6-Schedule w/resource assignments, logical sequences & applied constraints/milestones to drive execution targets.
    Level-6-Schedule shall be developed in full compliance w/TA-Scheduling-Premises issued by NSRP.
+ Finalizing Critical & near-Critical paths in Level-6-Schedule for each Package & identifying Longest path for each Unit.
+ Submitting a List of major tools & equipment, along w/Manpower histogram, to NSRP for review & approval prior to TA-Execution.
5.6.1g) ENERSEV shall develop Pre-SD WorkSchedule & baseline it no later than 3 months prior to TA SD-date. Progress shall be monitored, w/regular reports submitted to NSRP.
  NSRP SchedulingTeam shall manage & consolidate all Package & Area schedules for overall progress reporting. Mitigation measures for potential delays shall be prepares & readily available.
  All TA-Contractor-Schedulers shall be demonstrably competent & are preferably required to hold valid certifications in PrimaveraP6 & PowerBi
5.6.1h) During ExecutionPhase, if any activity is delayed, particularly those on Critical or near-Critical paths, ENERSEV shall implement recovery measures to prevent impact on overall TA-baseline duration.
5.6.1i) TA-IMS shall cover all TA-phases, including Pre-SD, SD, MechanicalExecution, StartUp & Post-SD.
  IMS shall be consolidated by NSRP's TA-Scheduling-Team following review & optimization & shall be established as a mutual agreed baseline for monitoring all works.
5.6.1j) TA-IMS shall include all activities undertaken by NSRP, TA-Contractors, 3rd-Parties & EPC-Projects.
  IMS shall be baselined upon mutual agreement between NSRP & ENERSEV & shall serve as Basis for progress monitoring, resource planning, delay analysis & reporting during TA-Execution.
  IMS shall be managed & controlled by NSRP's TA-Scheduling-Team.
